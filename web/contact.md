# Contact

canimcp is maintained in the open at <https://github.com/olaservo/canimcp>.

- To report an error in the registry, or a client that has changed, [open an issue](https://github.com/olaservo/canimcp/issues). Include the client version and what you saw. A correction that cites a commit or a capture is the quickest to apply.
- To ask for a client to be added, open an issue with a link to its source or its documentation.
- To report a problem with the site, the JSON under `/data` or the MCP server at `/mcp`, open an issue with the URL and the response you got.

There is no contact form and no mailing list. [About](/about) covers where the data comes from, and [Privacy](/privacy) covers what the site records.
